# Tenants

A tenant is a `Tenant` object of the `phorge.fr` group in `phorge-system` (`internal/kube/tenants.go`); Crossplane makes its namespace `t-<name>`, its Incus project, and its network.

- **Create** is for any person signed in with Authentik, for themselves: the owner is the caller (`owner` is left out or is their own email, another one is `403`), every tenant has the same limits (2 instances, 2 vCPU, 4 GiB of memory, 128 GiB of disk, 2 networks and 2 profiles, see [Limits and quotas](limits-and-quotas.md)) so a body leaves `plan` out, and a person owns at most `MAX_TENANTS_PER_USER` tenants (`409`, `quota_exceeded`, counted by owner, whoever created them). A key never creates a tenant. `GET /v1/whoami` says `canCreateTenants`. It answers `202` with a `Location`: poll it until `phase` is `Ready`. `phase` is `Provisioning`, `Ready`, `Deleting`, or `Failed` when the tenant has not become ready for 3 minutes and either `problems` says what is wrong in words made for the user (a limit that is reached, or that the platform could not complete a step yet) or the platform could not apply what makes the tenant for 3 minutes (the same rule holds for every kind: an object whose composition fails is `Failed` with a sentence that the platform administrators can see why, instead of staying `Provisioning`). It never carries what Crossplane, a provider or Incus report: those stay in `status.problems` of the object, for the administrators.
- **Patch** changes `displayName`: the admins of the tenant, the owner among them, do. The `plan` is deprecated and changes no limit, since every tenant has the same ones: a body that gives it is `403`, as it always was. A key never patches a tenant unless it holds `tenants:write`. The owner cannot change (it is an OpenFGA tuple): a body that holds it is refused. To hand a tenant over, give the new person the `admin` role with a membership.
- **Delete** is for the admins of the tenant, the owner among them. It removes what the tenant holds first (its instances, then its memberships, then its networks, then the profiles its users made, then its images), then the tenant, all in the foreground: `202`, then the tenant stays as `Deleting` until everything it is made of is gone (about two minutes, longer with a virtual machine to stop), and a `404` on a later get means the person no longer sees the tenant: it is gone or about to be, since the rights of the person (the owner tuple) go with the tenant a little before its last resources do (measured: the `404` came while the tenant was still `Deleting`). Stop polling on it, and do not take it for proof that Incus is empty. It destroys the virtual machines of the tenant. The order is not cosmetic: the platform deletes what a tenant holds in its namespace only while the namespace exists, so the tenant waits for them (see the Hangar issue 185).
- Whoever cannot read a tenant is told it does not exist (`404`), whoever can but may not do this gets `403`. Bodies are JSON only, 64 KiB at most, with no field the API does not know.
- **The `plan` is deprecated.** There is no choice of plan: every tenant has the same limits, whatever its `plan`. The field stays in the body of a tenant (`small` for a new one, what the object holds for an existing one) and in the answer of `GET /v1/tenants/{t}/quota`, only so that what reads it keeps working until the console stops using it; it is then removed from the API. A creation without a plan works, and a plan other than `small` on creation or on change is `403`.
